# Band Quest — Character Portrait Generation Prompts

These are ready-to-use prompts for generating the **11 class portraits** with an
image model (Midjourney, DALL·E 3, Imagen, SDXL, Nano Banana, etc.). Each portrait
is the hero art for one playable class. Generate, then drop the result into
`bandquest/public/portraits/<instrument>.png` and the app picks it up automatically
(see `docs/PORTRAITS.md`).

---

## Shared style preamble (paste this in front of EVERY class prompt)

> High-quality hand-painted 2D anime/JRPG character portrait, in the combined style of
> **One-Punch Man's** confident expressive linework and **Final Fantasy VI / VII Remake's**
> atmospheric painterly rendering. Bust / upper-body framing, character facing slightly
> 3/4 toward the viewer. **Highly detailed, realistically structured face**: defined nose
> with visible bridge and nostrils, full lips with distinct upper and lower lip and a soft
> philtrum, sculpted chin and jawline, soft cheek and jowl modelling, individually rendered
> eyelashes, shaped eyebrows with hair detail, and deep expressive eyes with iris texture,
> catchlights, and a subtle limbal ring. **Painterly soft shading with clear contour lines,
> form shadows, ambient occlusion, rim lighting, and a defined single key light** from the
> upper left. Smooth skin gradients, not flat fills. Subtle subsurface warmth in the skin.
> Clean dramatic three-quarter lighting. Centered composition, head-and-shoulders, looking
> at or just past the camera. Plain softly-vignetted background that does not distract from
> the character. No text, no watermark, no border, no UI. Consistent roster: all portraits
> share the same rendering quality, lighting direction, and framing so they look like one
> cast.

Then append ONE of the per-class blocks below.

## Tips for a consistent roster

- Generate at a **square or 4:5 aspect ratio**, then crop to a consistent head-and-shoulders.
- Keep the **same key-light direction (upper left)** across all 11 so the cast matches.
- If your model supports it, reuse a **style/character-reference** image of the first finished
  portrait you like as a seed for the rest to lock in rendering quality.
- Export PNG (transparent background preferred). Target ~1024×1024 or larger; the app
  downscales cleanly.
- Filenames must match the instrument id exactly:
  `flute, clarinet, alto_sax, trumpet, trombone, euphonium, percussion, french_horn, tuba, oboe, bassoon`.
